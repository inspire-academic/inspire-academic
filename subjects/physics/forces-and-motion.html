GCSE Physics: Forces and Motion

Build confidence describing motion with distance and displacement, then move through speed, motion graphs, resultant forces, free-body diagrams and Newton's laws — AQA and Edexcel, Higher and Foundation.

1 of 8 lessons built — benchmark preview
Study pathway:

Balanced and unbalanced forces

Topic Objectives

  • Distinguish distance (scalar) from displacement (vector) and explain why a round trip can have zero displacement.
  • Describe and analyse motion using speed, velocity and acceleration.
  • Understand forces, their effects, and how they combine into a resultant force.
  • Apply graphs, diagrams and equations to solve problems and explain real-world scenarios.
