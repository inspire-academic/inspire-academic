Ways to grow and protect your portfolio
Raise rents to market
Review rents annually at tenancy renewal. A phased increase aligned to market rates protects income without losing good tenants. Serve a Section 13 notice correctly.
EPC upgrades
Properties rated A–B command 10–15% higher rents and attract longer tenancies. Minimum C rating likely required by 2028. Boiler Upgrade Scheme grants available now.
Remortgage review
Review buy-to-let mortgages annually. Standard variable rates cost significantly more than fixed deals. A broker with whole-of-market access can find the best rates.
Expand the portfolio
Growing equity across your portfolio can be released via remortgage to fund a next purchase. Aim for properties with 6%+ yield in areas with strong rental demand.
Consider HMO
Larger properties can yield 20–40% more as licensed HMOs. Requires council licensing, fire safety works, and more active management. High yield for the right property.
Insurance review
Portfolio landlord policies are often cheaper than individual policies. Ensure you have buildings, loss of rent, liability, and legal expenses cover. Review annually.
