GeometryPythagoras' theorem

A right-angled triangle has a base of 4 cm and a height of 3 cm, as shown. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

A 5 cm
B 7 cm
C 12 cm
D 25 cm
Correct: A — √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 cm
GeometryIsosceles triangles

Triangle ABC is isosceles, with AB = AC. Angle A = 40°. What is the size of angle B?

A 40°
B 70°
C 100°
D 140°
Correct: B — base angles are equal: (180° − 40°) ÷ 2 = 70°
GeometryParallelograms

ABCD is a parallelogram. AB is parallel to DC, and AD is parallel to BC. Angle A = 65°. What is the size of angle B?

A 65°
B 90°
C 115°
D 130°
Correct: C — adjacent angles in a parallelogram are supplementary: 180° − 65° = 115°
Circle theoremsHigher

A and B are the ends of a diameter of the circle. C is a point on the circumference. What is the size of angle ACB?

A 45°
B 60°
C 90°
D It depends on where C is
Correct: C — the angle in a semicircle is always 90°
GraphsQuadratic graphs

The graph of y = x² − 4 is shown. What are the coordinates of the points where the curve crosses the x-axis?

A (2, 0) and (−2, 0)
B (4, 0) and (−4, 0)
C (0, 4) and (0, −4)
D (0, 0) only
Correct: A — x² − 4 = 0 gives x = ±2

The line y = 2x + 1 is shown. What is the gradient of the line?

A 1
B 2
C 1/2
D −2
Correct: B — in y = mx + c, m is the gradient
