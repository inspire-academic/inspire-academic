INSTRUCTIONAL STRATEGY

01

COMPRESSION PHILOSOPHY

Each lesson follows a strict 4-phase cycle: ACTIVATE → ATTACK → ANALYSE → ARMOUR. No lesson exceeds the content a student can absorb in one focused 45-minute session. Practicals are sequenced by exam frequency — highest-yield first.

02

SEQUENCING LOGIC

Week 1 covers the 8 highest-frequency practicals (appearing in >60% of past papers). Week 2 covers remaining practicals + a mock sprint. Every lesson ends with a 'Killer Phrase Bank' — the exact language examiners reward.

03

THE 4-PHASE LESSON FORMAT

① ACTIVATE: Core theory in ≤5 bullet points — what you must know cold. ② ATTACK: Predicted exam question, written in Edexcel style with mark allocation. ③ ANALYSE: Full model answer + mark scheme breakdown with examiner commentary. ④ ARMOUR: Common mistakes, misconceptions, and exam strategy tips.

04

2-WEEK STUDY PLAN

Days 1–7: One lesson per day, practicals ranked by frequency (Lessons 1–8). Days 8–14: One lesson per day, completing the full required practical set (Lessons 9–15) + full mock sprint on Day 14. Grade 9 threshold: Answer every practical question with correct method, correct terminology, and correct analysis.
