This is not a catch-up programme.

The Year 6 Science Bridging Programme is a serious, forward-looking transition — built for pupils who are ready to go deeper before they arrive, not for pupils who have fallen behind. Its purpose is confidence and curiosity: the difference between a pupil who walks into their first secondary school science lesson uncertain, and one who walks in already comfortable thinking like a scientist.

Who it's for: Year 6 pupils preparing for the move to secondary school science — no prior struggle required. The programme runs from February to August, spanning the run-up to that transition.

How the journey works

01

02

03

Teaching

Four projects. One journey.

Each project builds a different way of thinking scientifically — together, they form a complete foundation for secondary school science.

01

Great Science Investigator

Builds the core of scientific thinking — asking good questions, designing a fair test, and drawing a real conclusion from evidence.

02

Kitchen Chemistry

Chemistry through the everyday — familiar materials become the starting point for understanding reactions and change.

03

Invisible Worlds — Electricity, Energy, Systems & Connections

Physics as systems thinking — how energy moves, how circuits connect, and how the invisible parts of the world fit together.

04

Living Systems

Biology from the cell to the ecosystem — how living things are organised, and how they depend on one another.

Common questions

Is this a remedial programme?

No. It's built for pupils who are ready to go further, not for pupils who need to catch up. The focus is confidence and depth, not repair.

What is the time commitment?

The programme runs February to August, with periodic check-ins and coaching sessions built around the four projects rather than a single fixed weekly class.

What happens after August?

Pupils move into secondary school science with a genuine head start — comfortable with scientific thinking, not just familiar with the vocabulary.

Who teaches the programme?

Inspire Academic's science coaching team, using the same project-based approach across all four projects.
