Your child may not need "more tutoring." They need to know exactly what is holding them back.

Knowledge gaps

Small weaknesses compound quickly across GCSE Maths and the sciences. ISM identifies the gaps before simply adding more content.

Exam execution

Knowing the topic is not the same as converting knowledge into marks. Students practise application, calculations, explanations and exam technique.

No clear system

Four demanding subjects can become fragmented. ISM gives students a repeatable mastery pathway: learn, practise, assess, receive feedback, improve.

Teach deeply. Diagnose precisely. Intervene where it matters.

1 — Mastery Teaching.

Structured live instruction focused on the concepts and methods students must genuinely understand.

2 — Mastery Clinic.

A weekly shared learning touchpoint for difficult questions, misconceptions and exam-style problem solving.

3 — Diagnostic Practice.

Students practise and are assessed so support is based on evidence, not guesswork.

4 — Targeted Intervention.

Where a student needs more support, the programme can direct them into focused small-group intervention rather than repeating everything.

5 — Parent Visibility.

Parents receive meaningful information about progress, gaps and recommended next steps.
