3Core Lesson

1. Concentration is a ratio

A solution contains a solute dissolved in a solvent. Concentration tells us the mass or amount of solute in each unit volume of the final solution.

concentration (g/dm³) = mass of solute (g) ÷ volume of solution (dm³)

Volume conversion: 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³, so divide cm³ by 1000 before using these formulae.

2. Rearrange before substituting

Find mass

mass (g) = concentration (g/dm³) × volume (dm³)

Find volume

volume (dm³) = mass (g) ÷ concentration (g/dm³)

3. Molar concentration Higher

concentration (mol/dm³) = amount (mol) ÷ volume (dm³)

Use moles = mass ÷ Mr. Therefore g/dm³ = mol/dm³ × Mr, and mol/dm³ = g/dm³ ÷ Mr.

4Representations

Convert volume first

250 cm³ ÷ 1000 = 0.250 dm³

Never use 250 directly in a formula whose volume unit is dm³.

Reacting-volume route Higher

known c,V → known moles → equation ratio → unknown moles → unknown c

For HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O, the ratio is 1:1. 25.0 cm³ of 0.200 mol/dm³ HCl contains 0.00500 mol, so it reacts with 0.00500 mol NaOH.

5Worked Examples

Example 1 — find concentration

Data
12.0 g solute in 300 cm³ solution.
Convert
300 ÷ 1000 = 0.300 dm³.
Calculate
12.0 ÷ 0.300 = 40.0 g/dm³.
Common error
12 ÷ 300 uses the wrong volume unit.

Example 2 — find required mass

Data
250 cm³ of 24 g/dm³ solution.
Convert
250 cm³ = 0.250 dm³.
Calculate
24 × 0.250 = 6.0 g.
Sense check
0.250 dm³ is one quarter of a dm³, so it contains one quarter of 24 g.

Example 3 — molar concentration Higher

Data
5.85 g NaCl (Mr = 58.5) in 500 cm³.
Moles
5.85 ÷ 58.5 = 0.100 mol.
Volume
500 cm³ = 0.500 dm³.
Calculate
0.100 ÷ 0.500 = 0.200 mol/dm³.

6Misconception Clinic

“Concentration is the same as mass.”

Concentration is mass or amount per unit volume. The same mass can make different concentrations.

“Use cm³ directly.”

Convert cm³ to dm³ by dividing by 1000 when concentration uses per dm³.

“More solution always means more concentrated.”

Concentration depends on both solute and final volume. Adding solvent lowers concentration.

“g/dm³ and mol/dm³ are interchangeable.”

They measure different quantities. Convert using Mr.
