Know what is being learned. Know what comes next.

Clear, parent-friendly curriculum guides alongside the official Department for Education curriculum documents on which they are based.

In England, the national curriculum generally specifies programmes of study by key stage rather than prescribing an identical topic sequence for every individual school year. Schools determine their own curriculum sequence within the statutory framework. Inspire's year guides therefore combine the official curriculum entitlement with a clearly identified year-specific progression lens.
