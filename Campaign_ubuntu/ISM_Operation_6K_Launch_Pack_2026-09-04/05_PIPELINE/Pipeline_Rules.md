# 100-FAMILY PIPELINE RULES

## Status vocabulary
NEW → CONTACTED → REPLIED → DIAGNOSTIC_SENT → DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE → REVIEW_BOOKED → OFFERED → PAID  
Also: NURTURE / NOT_NOW / NOT_FIT / LOST

## Daily operating minimum
- 20 new or renewed direct contacts
- 5 meaningful follow-ups
- Same-day response to warm replies where practical
- Every lead has a next action and next-action date
- Every paid enrolment has source + monthly value recorded

## First 100 source mix
Suggested starting allocation:
- 30 current/previous Inspire families
- 20 direct personal/community relationships
- 20 parent referrals
- 15 church/community partner introductions
- 15 organic/social enquiries

Do not wait until all 100 names are found. Contact the first 20 while building the rest.

## Friday numbers
Count:
1. prospects contacted
2. replies
3. diagnostic starts
4. diagnostic completions
5. parent reviews
6. offers
7. paid students
8. new MRR
9. cash collected
10. acquisition spend
11. tutor/delivery commitments created

The bottleneck determines next week's work.
