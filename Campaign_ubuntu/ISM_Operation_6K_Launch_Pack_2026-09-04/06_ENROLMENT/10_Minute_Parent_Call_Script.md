# ISM 10-MINUTE PARENT ENROLMENT CALL

## Objective
Understand the parent's concern, explain the diagnostic finding, recommend the correct pathway and ask clearly for enrolment. This is not a 45-minute free tutoring consultation.

## Minute 0–2 — Why now?
“Thanks for making the time. Before I tell you what I noticed, what is the main thing worrying you about [Child] in Maths or Science at the moment?”

Listen. Write down their exact language.

Useful follow-ups:
- “When did you first notice that?”
- “Which subject causes the most difficulty?”
- “What would you most like to be different by Christmas?”

## Minute 2–5 — Findings
“From what we have seen so far, there are three things I would pay attention to.”

Give:
1. one genuine strength
2. one or two priority gaps
3. the consequence if the gap is left unattended

Do not exaggerate or frighten.

## Minute 5–7 — Prescription
“My recommendation is [Core/Plus], and the reason is…”

Explain only the parts of ISM that solve the parent's stated problem:
- live mastery teaching
- weekly clinic
- structured practice
- diagnostic tracking
- targeted intervention
- parent visibility

## Minute 7–8 — Price
“The [Core/Plus] membership is £[199/249] per calendar month. If you’re happy with the recommendation, I can secure [Child]’s place and send the enrolment link now.”

Then stop talking.

## Minute 8–10 — Handle the real objection

### “I need to think about it.”
“Of course. What specifically would you like to think through — the programme itself, whether it is right for [Child], the timetable, or the cost?”

### “It’s expensive.”
“I understand. The important question is whether the programme gives [Child] enough structured support across the subjects to justify that investment. If the issue is that the programme is not a fit, I would rather tell you that. If it is primarily budget, let’s identify that clearly rather than pretending it is something else.”

Do not immediately discount.

### “We already have a tutor.”
“That may be perfectly compatible. The question is what the existing tutor is solving. ISM is designed as a broader mastery system across Maths and Science with diagnostics, practice, clinic support and progress visibility. If the current arrangement already provides what [Child] needs, I would not ask you to duplicate it.”

### “Can we try one lesson?”
“We start with the diagnostic because a random lesson tells us very little. Once I understand [Child]’s needs, I can recommend the right pathway rather than giving you a generic trial.”

### “Can you guarantee a grade?”
“No responsible educator can guarantee a GCSE grade. What I can promise is a structured programme, careful diagnosis, high-quality teaching, practice, feedback and intervention designed to give [Child] a much stronger route to progress.”

## Close
“Would you like me to secure the place?”

If yes: send payment/enrolment link **during the call**.
If not: agree one exact follow-up date.

## After every call
Log:
- parent's exact concern
- objection
- recommended tier
- outcome
- next action
- next date

After 10 calls, rewrite landing-page FAQ using the objections you actually heard.
