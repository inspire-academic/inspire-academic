# COMMERCIAL + EDUCATIONAL GUARDRAILS

Before scaling:
- Confirm which legal entity contracts with parents and receives tuition payments.
- Confirm VAT/tax treatment with a qualified UK accountant for the actual legal/delivery structure.
- Publish clear terms covering recurring payments, cancellation, missed sessions, holidays, refunds and safeguarding.
- Do not claim guaranteed grades, validated predicted grades, or evidence you do not possess.
- Obtain consent and handle children's data in line with your privacy/safeguarding framework.
- Define maximum delivery capacity for Plus intervention before selling large numbers of Plus places.
- Keep CIC and Ltd money, contracts, services and related-party arrangements properly documented where both entities are involved.
- Track **profit**, not merely revenue: tutor cost, payment fees, advertising, software, admin, refunds and founder delivery time.

## Evidence rationale behind the model
Current EEF guidance describes small-group tuition as typically 2–5 pupils and reports an average +4 months' progress; it also emphasises targeting tuition using diagnostic assessment and training/support for tutors. The launch pack therefore uses diagnostics + targeted small-group intervention rather than promising unlimited 1:1 support.

This is an implementation rationale, not a claim that ISM itself has been independently evaluated or will produce a particular number of months' progress.
