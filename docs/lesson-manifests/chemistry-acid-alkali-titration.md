## LEARNING OBJECTIVES

- Set up and use a burette, volumetric pipette and conical flask safely and accurately.
- Identify a suitable endpoint and explain the role of the indicator and white tile.
- Calculate titres, select concordant accurate results and calculate a mean.
- Explain controls that reduce parallax, transfer and endpoint errors.
- Higher/AQA and calculation route: calculate an unknown concentration from reacting volumes and a balanced equation.

Central question: **How can we measure the exact volume needed for neutralisation?**

## PREREQUISITES

- Convert cm³ to dm³.
- Use amount = concentration × volume.
- Interpret balanced-equation mole ratios.
- Use the published **Concentration of Solutions** lesson.

## SPECIFICATION MAPPING

| Board | Authoritative scope |

| AQA Chemistry 8462 | §4.4.2.5 and Required Practical 2: strong acid–strong alkali reacting volumes; Higher-only concentration calculations in mol/dm³ and g/dm³ |
| Pearson Edexcel Chemistry | 5.9C accurate acid–alkali titration using burette, pipette and suitable indicator; 5.10C calculations from titration results |

The lesson stays with strong acid–strong alkali titration. It does not expand into pH curves, weak-acid equilibria or instrumental analysis.
