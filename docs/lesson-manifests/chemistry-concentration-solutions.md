## LEARNING OBJECTIVES

- Explain concentration as mass or amount of solute per unit volume of final solution.
- Convert between cm³ and dm³.
- Calculate concentration, mass and volume in g/dm³.
- Higher: calculate and convert concentrations in mol/dm³.
- Higher: use reacting volumes and mole ratios to calculate an unknown concentration.

Central question: **How much solute is present in each unit volume of solution?**

## PREREQUISITES

- Rearrange a three-variable equation.
- Calculate amount in moles from mass and M\(_r\).
- Interpret balanced-equation mole ratios.
