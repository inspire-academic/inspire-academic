## Sound-pathway decision (for Eric)

The lesson uses the four pathways of the approved concept card: mechanically, electrically, by heating, by radiation. Sound is described as a mechanical transfer ("a vibrating object does work on the air"). Pupils are taught to write "a small amount of energy is transferred by sound to the surroundings", which AQA mark schemes credit, and never "sound energy". The reviewer judged this defensible, provided "by sound" is never penalised, and that is now the case.
