# Electrolysis WCAG 2.2 AA internal audit

Date: 2026-08-31

Audited lesson: `teaching-lessons/chemistry/chemical-changes-electrolysis.html`

Staging lesson ID: `032d728e-5eac-4604-9537-ebf218214f54`

Viewer: `student/lesson-viewer.html`

Audit base: `staging` at `ef2d687bd331088b2632025040276a83c3291eb4`

## Outcome

Internal readiness result: **conditional pass after the repairs in this branch**.

No WCAG conformance claim is issued by this document. The code-level, visual, keyboard, 320 CSS-pixel reflow and WCAG text-spacing checks completed here are clean after remediation, but a complete conformance claim still requires the outstanding assistive-technology and genuine 200% browser-zoom checks listed below.

The evaluation approach follows the intent of the W3C Website Accessibility Conformance Evaluation Methodology (WCAG-EM): define the scope, sample all distinct states in the single-page lesson, test applicable WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA requirements, record findings, and rerun regression checks.

## Scope and states checked

- Authenticated staging viewer and the embedded Electrolysis HTML lesson.
- Learn and Practice modes.
- Foundation and Higher pathway controls.
- Light and dark themes.
- Lesson navigation, question controls, feedback/status regions and the Quick Reminder dialog.
- Desktop Chrome on Windows, including a narrow 693 CSS-pixel iframe state.

## Confirmed findings and repairs

### 1. Light-theme text contrast

Two source-level failures were confirmed:

- small sidebar labels used `#66768a` on `#f4f1e9`: **4.11:1**, below the 4.5:1 AA threshold;
- text on light-theme gold controls and section numbers used `#17202b` on `#8b5b00`: **2.81:1**.

Repair:

- light `--soft` changed to `#596a7f`: **4.91:1** on the page background;
- a theme-specific `--gold-text` token was introduced;
- light gold text is now `#fffdfa`: **5.75:1**;
- dark gold text remains `#17202b`: **10.55:1**.

### 2. Embedded-frame accessible names

The viewer-generated iframes had no `title`, so assistive technology could not distinguish the embedded content by name.

Repair: non-empty, purpose-specific titles were added for video, interactive HTML lesson and document frames.

## Checks passed

- Document language and meaningful page titles are present.
- Exactly one H1 and a logical section-heading structure are present.
- Both instructional figures have substantive alternative text.
- Visible form controls have accessible names; answer fields are labelled.
- No duplicate IDs, broken ARIA references or positive `tabindex` values were found.
- Learn/Practice tab relationships and live status regions are exposed.
- All sampled keyboard-focus targets showed a visible 3px focus indicator.
- The Quick Reminder dialog takes focus on open, traps Tab, closes with Escape and returns focus to its trigger.
- No visible target in the sampled states was smaller than 24 by 24 CSS pixels.
- A dedicated same-origin harness confirmed no horizontal lesson overflow at exactly 320 CSS pixels in Learn and Practice, Foundation and Higher, and dark and light states.
- The WCAG text-spacing override (1.5 line height, 2em paragraph spacing, 0.12em letter spacing and 0.16em word spacing) produced no content or control clipping at the 693 CSS-pixel audit width in every sampled state. The only deliberately clipped element was the existing 1px visually-hidden live-status region.
- Dark-theme visible text passed the computed contrast sweep; confirmed light-theme failures are repaired and regression-tested.
- Reduced-motion handling is present; no autoplay, flashing content, time limits or drag-only interactions were found.
- Full committed suite: **355/355 passed** after adding two focused accessibility tests.

## Outstanding evidence before a formal AA claim

- Run an end-to-end task pass with a current desktop screen reader (NVDA with Chrome is the recommended Windows combination), including headings, landmarks, figures, tabs, question feedback and dialog announcements.
- Verify at actual 200% browser zoom, including both lesson modes and both pathways. The connected Chrome control can operate page content but cannot change Chrome's browser-level zoom setting.
- Repeat a short keyboard and screen-reader smoke test after the repaired viewer and lesson are deployed, because the staging URL audited here still serves the pre-repair build.

These are evidence gaps, not known product defects. They prevent this internal report from being presented as a completed formal WCAG 2.2 AA conformance determination.

## Regression coverage

`tests/lesson-a11y-structural.test.js` now verifies:

- both theme-specific gold foreground/background pairs remain at or above 4.5:1;
- the light small-label pair remains at or above 4.5:1;
- the relevant gold-filled components use the audited token;
- every iframe generated by the lesson viewer has a non-empty accessible title.
