## Why this lesson is next

This is the most coherent lesson after Acid–Alkali Titration. Both are Chemical Changes required-practical lessons, but they teach complementary preparation routes: titration for two soluble reactants, and excess insoluble solid plus filtration for making a soluble salt. The sequence strengthens method selection without repeating Titration.

## Locked scope

Included: choosing the acid and insoluble solid; copper(II) oxide plus dilute sulfuric acid; gentle warming; adding solid to excess; filtration; careful evaporation; cooling to crystallise; filtering and drying crystals; apparatus, safety, purity and error analysis.

Excluded: titration, pH curves, solubility curves, hydrated-salt calculations, qualitative ion tests and industrial crystallisers.
